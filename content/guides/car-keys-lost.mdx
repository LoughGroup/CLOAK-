Lost or stolen car keys can quickly turn into a vehicle theft risk, especially if the key was taken near where the car is parked.

## Step 1 — Locate the vehicle and confirm it hasn’t been taken

Go to where the vehicle was last parked and confirm it’s still there. If it’s not, first rule out towing or relocation (building management, council, or a tow company) before you assume theft. If you believe the theft is in progress or just occurred, call **000**.

## Step 2 — Arrange a replacement key (dealer or auto locksmith)

Contact your car dealer or a reputable **auto locksmith** to organise a replacement key. Provide the make/model/year and be ready to show proof of ownership and ID. Ask about expected timeframe, cost, and whether they can attend your location if the vehicle can’t be moved.

## Step 3 — Report to police if the keys were stolen

If the keys were stolen (from a bag, home, workplace, or burglary), make a report with **police** and keep the event/reference number. This helps with insurance and supports later disputes if the car is stolen using the key. For non-urgent reporting, **131 444** is commonly used depending on your state/territory.

## Step 4 — Notify your insurer

Call your insurer and explain whether the keys were lost or stolen and whether the vehicle’s location is known. Ask what they require if the car is later stolen, and whether any costs for towing or locksmith services are covered. Document the timeline of when the keys went missing.

## Step 5 — Remove the lost key from the vehicle’s system (reprogram/reset)

Ask the dealer/locksmith whether the car’s keyless entry or immobiliser system should be **reprogrammed** to delete the missing key from memory. This is especially important if the key was stolen and could be used to drive away. If you have a spare key, keep it secured at home until reprogramming is complete.
