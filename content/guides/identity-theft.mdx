If you believe your identity has been misused in Australia, act quickly to limit fraud and account takeover. The steps below are a practical order to follow.

## Step 1 — Contact IDCARE

**IDCARE** (Australia and New Zealand’s national identity and cyber support service) can help you plan a response and understand what to do next.

- **Phone:** 1800 595 160 (free call)  
- **Website:** [idcare.org](https://www.idcare.org)

They can walk you through containment and recovery tailored to your situation.

## Step 2 — Place a credit ban

Ask **Equifax** and **illion** to place a **ban** (sometimes called a credit freeze) on your credit file so new credit accounts are harder to open in your name. Check each bureau’s website for the fastest online form, or call their listed support numbers.

Keep confirmation details and note how long each ban lasts and how to extend it if needed.

## Step 3 — Report to police

Report the identity theft to your **local police** (online or in person, depending on your state or territory). Request an **event or reference number** — banks, telcos, and insurers often need it.

## Step 4 — Lock down MyGov and alert the ATO

Sign in to **myGov** from a trusted device, review linked services and contact details, and strengthen your sign-in options. If you cannot access myGov or suspect it was compromised, use official **Services Australia** channels for help.

Contact the **ATO** if you are worried about tax fraud or unauthorised changes to your tax record — use only the phone numbers and links published on **ato.gov.au**.
