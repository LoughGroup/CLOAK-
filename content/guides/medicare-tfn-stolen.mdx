If a Medicare card or TFN is stolen, assume it may be used for identity fraud and act quickly to get the right agencies to flag it.

## Step 1 — Report Medicare card theft to Services Australia

Contact **Services Australia** and report the Medicare card stolen so they can advise the fastest path to secure and reissue it. Ask what steps apply to your specific situation (lost card vs identity compromise) and keep any reference numbers.  

132 011

## Step 2 — Request a new Medicare card number

Ask Services Australia whether they can **issue a new Medicare card number** (not just a replacement plastic card) if you suspect the number is exposed. Update the new number with regular providers once issued, and review recent Medicare activity in your linked services. If you also use myGov, check Medicare-linked details there for changes.

## Step 3 — Report TFN theft to the ATO

Call the **ATO** and tell them you believe your TFN has been stolen or misused. Ask them to place appropriate flags and explain what monitoring they recommend for your circumstances.  

132 861

## Step 4 — Lodge a tax fraud alert and monitor your record

Ask the ATO about a **tax fraud alert** and what to watch for (unexpected notices, new bank accounts added, refund changes, new agent access). Keep a written log of dates and who you spoke to. If you have a tax agent, notify them that your TFN may be compromised.

## Step 5 — Contact IDCARE for identity-risk support

Contact **IDCARE** for tailored guidance on identity theft containment, credit reporting, and what to do if other documents were involved. They can help you plan the order of next actions based on your exact exposure.  

1800 595 160

## Step 6 — Review myGov and key account security

Sign in to **myGov** from a trusted device and check linked services for contact-detail changes, new devices, or unfamiliar activity. Update passwords on your primary email account and enable stronger multi-factor methods where possible. If you see active fraud, make a police report and keep the event number for follow-ups.
