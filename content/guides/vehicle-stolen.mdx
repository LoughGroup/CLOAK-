If your vehicle is missing, the priority is to confirm what happened and get a police report number quickly for insurance and recovery.

## Step 1 — Confirm it’s stolen and not towed

Check nearby streets, signage, and any building parking notices in case it was relocated. Contact your local council and nearby towing companies to confirm it wasn’t towed for parking or safety reasons. If it’s not accounted for, treat it as stolen and move to the next step.

## Step 2 — Call police immediately

If the theft has just occurred or you’re witnessing it, call **000**. Otherwise report the theft via your local police reporting channel or call **131 444** (non-urgent). Provide the registration number, make/model, colour, VIN (if you have it), and the last known location/time.

## Step 3 — Report to your insurer

Call your insurer as soon as you have the police event/reference number and start a claim. Ask what evidence they need (photos, service history, finance details) and what to do if the car is recovered damaged. Keep notes of who you spoke to and claim/reference numbers.

## Step 4 — Notify roads authority if rego papers were inside

If registration papers or other identifying documents were in the vehicle, notify your state/territory roads authority and ask what needs to be replaced or flagged. This reduces the chance someone uses those papers to misrepresent ownership or change details. Update any address or contact info at the same time if needed.

## Step 5 — List valuables or documents that were inside

Write down any valuables, garage remotes, IDs, or mail that were in the vehicle. This helps with your insurance claim and highlights whether follow-on actions are needed (e.g. change garage codes, rekey home locks, replace identity documents). If identity documents were inside, consider contacting **IDCARE** (1800 595 160).
