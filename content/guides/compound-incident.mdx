If several important items are missing at once, the key is to slow down and work in a clear order that prevents follow-on account takeover.

## Step 1 — Stop and breathe (then write a quick list)

Take 60 seconds to steady yourself and write down what’s missing and when/where you last had it. This prevents duplicated calls and helps you give consistent information to banks, police, and insurers. Keep a single note with reference numbers as you go.

## Step 2 — Start with your phone (it controls two-factor for everything else)

If your phone is lost or stolen, secure it first: block the SIM with your carrier and use Find My / Find My Device to lock or wipe if needed. If you suspect a SIM swap or port, contact your telco immediately and ask for a port freeze. Once the phone risk is contained, move to accounts that rely on SMS codes.

## Step 3 — Then lock down cards and bank access

Use your banking app to **freeze cards** (if possible) and call your bank’s fraud/lost-cards line to cancel compromised cards and watch for unauthorised transfers. Ask them to add a note or extra verification to your profile if identity documents were also lost. Keep transaction screenshots and call reference numbers.

## Step 4 — Then handle identity documents (licence, passport, Medicare, TFN)

Report stolen identity documents to the relevant issuer and request replacements or flags. If your driver licence or Medicare details were taken, place credit alerts/bans with the bureaus and monitor myGov-linked services for changes. The goal is to reduce the chance of new accounts being opened using your documents.

## Step 5 — Use the individual guides for each item (this is your starting point only)

Once the immediate containment is done, open the specific guide for each missing item and follow through to completion (police report numbers, insurer claims, replacement processes). Working guide-by-guide prevents important steps from being missed. Keep the sequence consistent: contain access, create a record, then recover.

## Step 6 — Contact IDCARE if documents were also lost

If identity documents were involved or you see evidence of misuse, contact **IDCARE** for tailored advice and next steps.
