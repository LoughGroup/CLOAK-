A stolen laptop can expose saved passwords and work data, so focus first on remote actions and account security.

## Step 1 — Remote lock or wipe the device

If it’s an Apple device, use your Apple ID tools or Find My (see [appleid.apple.com](https://appleid.apple.com) and [icloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find)) to lock or erase if appropriate. For Windows devices, check [account.microsoft.com/devices](https://account.microsoft.com/devices) for remote management options depending on how the device is configured. If the device is managed by your workplace, follow IT’s instructions before wiping so you don’t disrupt recovery processes.

## Step 2 — Notify your IT department or employer immediately

Report the theft to your employer/IT team as soon as possible, especially if the device is work-issued or has company access. Ask them to revoke device sessions, rotate credentials, and disable access tokens for email/VPN/SSO. Provide the device type, serial/asset tag, and the last known location/time.

## Step 3 — Change passwords saved on the device

Assume any credentials saved in the browser, password manager, or email client could be at risk if the device is accessed. Start with your **email** and any accounts that can reset others (banking, myGov, SSO). Revoke active sessions where available and enable stronger multi-factor (authenticator app or passkeys) on high-value accounts.

## Step 4 — Report the theft to police

Report to **police** (online where available, or call **131 444** for non-urgent reporting) and obtain an event/reference number. Provide identifying details like serial number, device model, and any tracking information. Keep the report number for insurers and for your workplace if required.

## Step 5 — Notify your insurer and document what was accessible

Contact your insurer and provide receipts, photos, and the police event number. Separately, write down what data was accessible on the device (documents, client info, saved files, synced drives) so you can assess disclosure risk. If sensitive personal documents were stored locally, consider contacting **IDCARE** (1800 595 160).
