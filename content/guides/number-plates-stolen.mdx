Stolen number plates are often used in other offences, so create a record quickly and replace the plates to reduce your risk.

## Step 1 — Report to police immediately

Report the theft to **police** as soon as you notice the plates are missing and request an event/reference number. If your state allows online reporting for stolen property, use that; otherwise call **131 444** (non-urgent). Keep the report details handy in case tolls, fines, or incidents appear linked to your registration.

## Step 2 — Replace plates with your state roads authority

Contact your state or territory roads/transport authority to organise **replacement plates** and ask what documentation they need. Follow their instructions for surrendering remaining plates (if only one was taken) and updating your registration record. Ask how long it takes for replacement plates to become active in their systems.

## Step 3 — Notify your insurer

Tell your insurer that your plates were stolen and provide the police report number if requested. Ask whether any policy conditions require you to take additional steps, and whether they need updated plate details once replacements are issued. Document the date/time you first noticed the theft.

## Step 4 — Keep the police report number for disputes

If fines, toll notices, or incident reports arrive, your first defence is the **police report reference** showing when you reported the plates stolen. Respond promptly to any notices and provide the report number and replacement-plate details. Keep copies of all correspondence.
