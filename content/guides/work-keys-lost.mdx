Work keys can create real security exposure, so the goal is to get the right people involved quickly and limit access.

## Step 1 — Notify your employer or facilities manager immediately

Tell your manager, facilities, or security team as soon as you realise keys are missing. Share what keys were on the ring and where/when you last had them. Early notification gives security the best chance to respond before any misuse.

## Step 2 — Check whether a security lockdown or rekey is required

Ask what your workplace policy requires for lost keys and whether a **lockdown**, **temporary access control**, or **rekey** is needed. The decision depends on whether the keys are identifiable (labelled) and what doors they open. Confirm who is authorised to approve rekeying and what documentation is required.

## Step 3 — Report to police if stolen

If the keys were stolen (not simply misplaced), make a police report and keep the event/reference number. This supports internal incident records and helps if there’s later unauthorised access. For non-urgent reporting, your state may use online reporting or **131 444**.

## Step 4 — Document what the keys gave access to

Write down exactly what each key opens (external doors, offices, cabinets, storage areas, vehicles) and whether those areas contain sensitive assets. Include any alarm codes, swipe cards, or access tokens that were on the same ring. This list helps security decide what to change first.

## Step 5 — Contact your building manager if keys provide external access

If the keys include building master keys or external building access, notify the building manager/concierge so they can increase monitoring and decide on rekeying. Ask whether access logs, CCTV, or patrols can be reviewed around the time the keys went missing. Close the loop once replacement keys are issued and old keys are confirmed invalid.
