If your phone has been stolen in Australia, speed matters. The steps below minimise fraud, account takeover, and further loss.

## Step 1 — Call your telco (block the SIM)

Thieves can receive SMS codes and bypass some protections while your SIM is active.

- **Telstra:** 13 22 00  
- **Optus:** 1300 300 937  
- **Vodafone:** 1300 650 410  

Ask for an immediate **SIM block** or **suspension** and confirm whether a replacement SIM can be issued. If you use an eSIM, ask how to revoke it on your account.

## Step 2 — Remote lock and wipe

**iPhone (Find My):** On another device, open the **Find My** app or [icloud.com/find](https://www.icloud.com/find), select your iPhone, choose **Lost Mode** (lock + message), then **Erase iPhone** if recovery is unlikely or data is sensitive.

**Android (Find My Device):** Visit [google.com/android/find](https://www.google.com/android/find) while signed into the same Google account, secure the device, then use **Erase device** if needed.

Wiping does not replace a SIM block — do both.

## Step 3 — Police report

Report the theft to your **local police** (in person or online, depending on your state). Request a **reference or event number** — banks, insurers, and telcos often ask for it.

## Step 4 — Secure accounts in order

Work through these in sequence to cut off the highest-risk access paths:

1. **Email** — sign out other sessions, change password, review recovery email and phone numbers.  
2. **Banking** — call your bank’s fraud line, block cards in the app, and watch for transfers or payees added without your knowledge.  
3. **myGov** — sign in from a trusted device, review linked services, update your sign-in settings, and consider additional verification options offered by Services Australia.  
4. **Super** — contact your fund, confirm no changes to contact details or beneficiary nominations, and ask what they can flag for unusual access.

After this, reset passwords on other critical apps and enable **multi-factor authentication** everywhere it is offered.
